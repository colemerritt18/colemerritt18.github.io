Discover Trips

Discover Trips is the premier website to find your next vacation. Just answer this quick survey, and we will provide you with a comprehensive analysis of your preferred trip location. We will provide a list of recommended trip locations, along with a link to a trip itinerary, containing popular tourist attractions and activities to do based on the answers provided.

Our keys to finding the right vacation for you:

  1. First and foremost, your enjoyment. Your enjoyment, along with those you bring along with you should come first.
  2. Second, we take into account the variety of activities on a vacation that will suit your group's and your interests best.
  3. Last but not least, we consider the size and age of the group, as well as the specifics on the group's purpose (e.g. a family, business trip, couple, adult friends, seniors, young adults, etc.)
